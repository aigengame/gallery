VERDANT / 青野 — 0.1.1

中文
解压 Verdant-macOS.zip，打开 Verdant.app 即可游玩，无须 Godot 编辑器或 gda。
适用于 Apple Silicon 与 Intel Mac。本地构建使用临时签名，未作 Apple 公证。

WASD / 方向键：移动    鼠标：观察    Shift：快走
Ctrl：蹲下看草叶      空格：跳跃    E：记录观景点    Esc：暂停
沿着小径寻找三个木牌，靠近后按 E 记录。完成路线后可继续漫步或重新开始。

在设置中选择中文 / English、2560×1440 / 3840×2160、窗口 / 全屏、
草地质量、风动和音量。分辨率指真实内部渲染尺寸；较小屏幕会按比例显示。
默认 2K。4K 需要更强的 GPU；如移动不流畅，请降低草地质量或选用 2K。

反馈时请记下 Mac 型号、分辨率、画质、出现问题的位置和操作。
重点观察草叶近看是否自然、风动、距离过渡、鼠标移动，以及中英文界面。

English
Extract Verdant-macOS.zip and open Verdant.app. Godot and gda are not required.
This universal build supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. It is locally signed
and has not been notarized by Apple.

WASD / arrow keys: move    Mouse: look    Shift: sprint
Ctrl: crouch              Space: jump   E: record viewpoint    Esc: pause
Follow the paths to three wooden markers. Press E nearby to record each view.
After completing the route, keep exploring or restart.

Settings include English / Chinese, 2560×1440 / 3840×2160, windowed / fullscreen,
grass quality, wind, and volume. Resolution is the actual internal render size;
the image is fitted to smaller screens. The default is 2K. 4K needs a faster GPU.
Lower grass quality or select 2K if movement is not smooth.

When reporting feedback, include your Mac model, resolution, quality, location,
and actions. Check close grass detail, wind, distance transitions, mouse movement,
and both languages.
